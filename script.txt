TITLE: Caleb & Aria
PROJECT: Mock Scenario
VERSION: 02/24/2026

DISPUTANT 1 NAME: Caleb
DISPUTANT 1 PROFILE: 6th Grade Boy

DISPUTANT 2 NAME: Aria
DISPUTANT 2 PROFILE: 6th Grade Girl

MEDIATOR A NAME: Emily
MEDIATOR B NAME: Michael

CONFLICT DESCRIPTION: When music class was starting, Caleb and Aria started arguing about who should sit in a seat by the instruments cart while other students were watching. The music teacher had stepped into the storage room to grab drumsticks, and neither of them would move.
CONFLICT BEHAVIORS: Insulting, Arguing in class

WHAT HAPPENED (ACCORDING TO D1):
Caleb says that Aria called him “annoying” and said he always sits in the wrong spot. He also says he didn’t know Aria usually sits there and thought you could sit anywhere if you got there first.

WHAT HAPPENED (ACCORDING TO D2):
Aria says that Caleb took the seat she usually sits in during music class, and he wasn’t listening to her when she tried to tell him it was her normal spot.

WHAT D1 WAS MOST BOTHERED BY:
Caleb was bothered by being called out in front of the class.

WHAT D2 WAS MOST BOTHERED BY:
Aria was bothered by the fact that Caleb wouldn’t move and didn’t listen when she told him it was her regular seat.

WHAT D1 FELT:
Caleb felt put on the spot when Aria called him annoying while other students were watching.

WHAT D2 FELT:
Aria felt frustrated when Caleb stayed in the seat and ignored what she was saying.

WHAT D1 WANTS:
Caleb wants Aria to not insult him in front of other people.

WHAT D2 WANTS:
Aria wants Caleb to let her have her usual seat since it’s near her friends.

WHAT D1 CAN DO DIFFERENTLY:
Caleb can ask if someone usually sits in a seat before taking it and listen when someone explains something.

WHAT D2 CAN DO DIFFERENTLY:
Aria can explain calmly that it is her seat and not call names.

WHAT BOTH CAN DO DIFFERENTLY:
Both can use calm voices and ask the teacher for help instead of arguing.

AGREEMENT – D1:
Caleb agrees to move to a different open seat in music class if Aria asks to have a seat that is close to her friends.

AGREEMENT – D2:
Aria agrees to tell Caleb it’s her usual seat without calling him annoying or insulting him in front of others.
