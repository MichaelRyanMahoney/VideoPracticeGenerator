DISPUTANT 2 PROFILE: 6th Grade Boy

MEDIATOR A NAME: Maria
MEDIATOR B NAME: Alex

CONFLICT DESCRIPTION: During a history group project, Jordan started assigning roles to other students in the group. Daniel told Jordan to stop "trying to run everything" in front of the group, which led the two students to start arguing.
CONFLICT BEHAVIORS: Insulting, Arguing in class

WHAT HAPPENED (ACCORDING TO D1):
Jordan says he was trying to help the group finish before class ended, and no one else was stepping up to assign roles.

WHAT HAPPENED (ACCORDING TO D2):
Daniel says that Jordan started picking roles without asking anyone else, and told Daniel to print the outline for the project. Daniel says that his printer at home is broken.

WHAT D1 WAS MOST BOTHERED BY:
Jordan was bothered by getting called out in front of everyone.

WHAT D2 WAS MOST BOTHERED BY:
Daniel was bothered by Jordan deciding roles without checking with the group first.

WHAT D1 FELT:
Jordan felt mad when Daniel said he was trying to run everything.

WHAT D2 FELT:
Daniel felt irritated when Jordan told him he needed to print the outline.

WHAT D1 WANTS:
Jordan wants Daniel to not call him out in front of everyone during group work.

WHAT D2 WANTS:
Daniel wants Jordan to ask the group before picking roles and let people choose when possible.

WHAT D1 CAN DO DIFFERENTLY:
Jordan can ask the group before assigning roles and listen to what roles people want.

WHAT D2 CAN DO DIFFERENTLY:
Daniel can say what he thinks calmly instead of calling Jordan out in front of everyone.

WHAT BOTH CAN DO DIFFERENTLY:
Both can listen to each other and try to make decisions together.

AGREEMENT – D1:
Jordan agrees to ask the group before assigning roles so that group members can discuss which roles they want.

AGREEMENT – D2:
Daniel agrees to not call Jordan out or say negative things about him in front of the group.

AGREEMENT – BOTH:
Both Jordan and Daniel agree to go together to the library to print the outline if no one else in the group can print it.
