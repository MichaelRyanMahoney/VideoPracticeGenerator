MEDIATOR B NAME: Sara

CONFLICT DESCRIPTION: Monica shared a screenshot of a private group chat message that Christina sent. Another student showed Christina the screenshot at school, and other students started talking about her. Christina yelled at Monica in the hallway the next day.
CONFLICT BEHAVIORS: Posting or messaging

WHAT HAPPENED (ACCORDING TO D1):
Christina says Monica screenshotted a private Snapchat group chat and sent it to other people without asking, and someone showed it to her at school.

WHAT HAPPENED (ACCORDING TO D2):
Monica says she shared the screenshot with one friend who asked for proof and didn’t realize it would spread. She says that Christina yelled at her and called her fake in the hallway where other students could hear.

WHAT D1 WAS MOST BOTHERED BY:
Christina was bothered by her private message being screenshotted and sent around without her permission.

WHAT D2 WAS MOST BOTHERED BY:
Monica was bothered by getting yelled at in front of other students.

WHAT D1 FELT:
Christina felt betrayed when she saw the screenshot and realized people at school were talking about her behind her back.

WHAT D2 FELT:
Monica felt embarrassed when Christina yelled at her and called her fake in front of other students.

WHAT D1 WANTS:
Christina wants Monica to delete the screenshot and to not share her messages with other people.

WHAT D2 WANTS:
Monica wants Christina to talk to her privately and address the problem calmly instead of calling her out publicly.

WHAT D1 CAN DO DIFFERENTLY:
Christina can talk to Monica privately instead of yelling at her in the hallway.

WHAT D2 CAN DO DIFFERENTLY:
Monica can think before screenshotting messages and say no if friends ask for proof.

WHAT BOTH CAN DO DIFFERENTLY:
Both can try to respect each other’s privacy both online and in school.

AGREEMENT – D1:
Christina agrees to not yell or call Monica names and to talk to her one-on-one if something bothers her.

AGREEMENT – D2:
Monica agrees to delete the screenshot and to not share private messages again.
